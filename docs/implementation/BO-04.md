## Scope

BO-04 adds a physics-informed Stage 1 surrogate and completes the small Stage 1
comparison framework for:

- fixed grid;
- generic GP;
- physics-informed GP.

It does not add Stage 2 optimization, hybrid LLM behavior, production lab
recommendations, or paper-scale experiments.

## Physics-Informed Surrogate

The model is a GPyTorch exact GP with a trainable saturating mean:

```text
m(t) = g_inf * (1 - exp(-k * t))
```

`g_inf` and `k` are positive constrained trainable parameters. A stationary Matérn
residual GP models deviations around the saturating mean and provides predictive
uncertainty.

This is accurately a physics-informed GP with optimized physical parameters. It is
not a fully Bayesian posterior over `g_inf` and `k`.

## Fit Behavior

The custom physics-informed model uses a short deterministic GPyTorch Adam training
loop over the exact marginal log likelihood. Fit retries are supported. The
optimizer records warnings, and it can fall back to the generic GP when the
physics-informed fit fails.

## Fair Comparison

`Stage1RunnerSettings` now carries shared comparison settings for all Stage 1 methods:

- initial observations;
- experiment budget;
- candidate grid size;
- target and endpoint settings;
- simulator and optimizer seeds;
- noise streams through a shared seeded `Stage1VirtualLab`;
- minimum observations before recommendation.

The BO-04 virtual endpoint is evaluation-only: success means a tested dose reaches at
least 95% of true finite saturation and lies within the configured dose tolerance of
the minimum true threshold dose.

## Result Schema

Per-run summaries include:

- success and failure category;
- experiments used;
- estimated and true t95;
- absolute and relative t95 error;
- growth fraction at recommendation;
- dose overshoot;
- false-saturation flag;
- cumulative dose;
- cumulative simulated process time;
- uncertainty coverage;
- model-fit warning count;
- optimizer wall time;
- endpoint experiment ID.
